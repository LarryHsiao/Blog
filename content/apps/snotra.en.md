## ✨ Features

- 👥 **Contacts & groups** — sort clients into groups, each with its own follow-up interval in days
- ⏰ **Overdue reminders** — anyone past their interval lands in the reminder center, so you see who's gone quiet longest
- 📝 **Meeting records** — log the date, sort with quick tags, add a note
- 🎁 **Gift records** — keep a gift catalogue and remember what each person has received
- 📅 **Important dates** — birthdays and anniversaries, repeating yearly, with a reminder a few days ahead
- 🔔 **Reminder window** — choose when notifications may arrive; the text never includes a contact's name
- 🔒 **Private by default** — use it without an account and your data stays on your phone; no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting
- ☁️ **Optional sync** — sign in only when you want multiple devices or a new phone
- 💾 **Encrypted backup** — encrypted with a password you set, and kept by you
