I made a new app. It's called Snotra.

It's a customer relationship management app. It keeps your meetings and the gifts you've given on record, and nudges you when someone is due a follow-up.

### Why I made it

One person looks after a lot of clients. Dozens. Sometimes a hundred.

Remembering every one of them isn't possible. When did you last meet? What did you give them last time? Whose birthday is next month? It won't all fit in your head, and the notes are scattered everywhere.

So someone always ends up left behind. Not because you don't care. They just never made it to the front of the queue.

What I wanted was a tool that remembers who's due, so you can spend your energy on the meeting itself.

### What it does today

- 👥 Sort clients into groups, each with its own follow-up interval in days
- ⏰ Anyone past their interval lands in the reminder center, so you can see at a glance who's gone quiet longest
- 📝 Meeting records and 🎁 gift records: a date, tags, a note, and what you gave
- 📅 Important dates: birthdays, anniversaries, repeating yearly, with a reminder a few days ahead
- 🔒 No account needed. Your data stays on your phone until you want a new phone or several devices, and then you sign in to sync

### Where your data lives

This is your clients' information, so by default it lives only on your phone. No ads, no analytics. A notification never shows a client's name.

The details are in the [privacy policy](/snotra/privacy_policy).
